Problems in the style of GATE and university exams, each with a worked solution and a Delta
program that checks the answer. Change the program to try a variant: the checks tell you at once
whether your new answer is right.

| # | Problem | Topic | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [a's mod 3, b's mod 2](/practice/dfa-mod-counts/) | DFA, product construction | Numerical |
| 2 | [Binary numbers divisible by 5](/practice/divisible-by-5/) | DFA, minimization | Numerical |
| 3 | [Strings containing aa](/practice/regex-contains-aa/) | Regular expressions | MCQ |
| 4 | [Third symbol from the end](/practice/third-from-end/) | Subset construction | Numerical |
| 5 | [No two consecutive 1s](/practice/no-consecutive-ones/) | Counting, DFAs | Numerical |
| 6 | [A grammar's language](/practice/grammar-balanced/) | Context-free grammars | MCQ |
| 7 | [Tracing a Turing machine](/practice/tm-trace/) | Turing machines | Numerical |
| 8 | [A DFA and a regex](/practice/dfa-vs-regex/) | Equivalence | Two parts |

The ideas behind them are in [Learn](/learn/), and the [tools](/tools/) run each construction
on your own machines.
